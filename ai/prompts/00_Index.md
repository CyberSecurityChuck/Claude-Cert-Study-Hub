# Prompts — Copy-Paste Kit (Index)

Ready-to-paste prompts for every stage of your Claude Certified Architect – Foundations study
workflow. Open the file you need, copy the fenced prompt into any AI (free Claude.ai is fine), attach
the listed files, and go. The tutor follows `System_Prompt/AI_System_Prompt.md` and reads/updates
`Context/Progress_Tracker.md`.

| # | File | When to use |
|---|------|-------------|
| 01 | `01_First_Chat.md` | Your very first study session — start at the Step 0 primer. |
| 02 | `02_Pause_Session.md` | You need to stop for now — summarize + save your place. |
| 03 | `03_Resume_Chat.md` | New/fresh chat to continue exactly where you left off. |
| 04 | `04_Section_Quiz.md` | You finished a section — take (or have the AI author) the matching quiz in the Exam Center. |
| 05 | `05_Log_My_Scores.md` | You took an Exam Center quiz/mock — record scores + find weak areas. |
| 06 | `06_Mock_Test_Day.md` | Phase 2 (from 2026-09-14) — take today's timed 60-question mock in the Exam Center. |
| 07 | `07_Generate_New_Questions.md` | You want more practice — author a new quiz or mock that plugs into the Exam Center. |
| 08 | `08_Explain_Weak_Areas.md` | Targeted re-teaching of the concepts you keep missing. |

> **Tip:** Almost every session, attach `System_Prompt/AI_System_Prompt.md` **first** and
> `Context/Progress_Tracker.md` **second** so the AI knows who to be and where you are.
