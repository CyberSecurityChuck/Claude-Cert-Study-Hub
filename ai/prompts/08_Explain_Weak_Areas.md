# 08 — Explain My Weak Areas (targeted review)

**When to use:** After a quiz/mock when you keep missing certain topics and want focused re-teaching.

**Copy-paste prompt:**

```
From my recent wrong answers on <topics — e.g. "MCP servers vs clients, and running summaries">,
please re-teach JUST those concepts simply, one at a time, with a concrete example or cybersecurity
analogy for each. Assume I'm a total AI beginner and keep it plain.

Then give me 3 quick check questions in chat (one at a time — wait for my answer before the next, and
explain why each answer is right or wrong).
```

**Attach these files:**
- `System_Prompt/AI_System_Prompt.md`
- `Context/Progress_Tracker.md`
- The relevant `Knowledge/0X_*.md` file(s) for the weak topics
