# 05 — Log My Exam Center Scores

**When to use:** Right after you take a quiz or mock in the Exam Center. Exam Center scores live only in the
browser, so you must report them so they get saved into the tracker.

**Copy-paste prompt:**

```
Here are my Exam Center scores: <paste your scores here — e.g. "Domain 1 quiz: 8/10", or "Mock #1: 44/60,
estimated scaled 735">.

Please update Context/Progress_Tracker.md to record these (append to the checkpoint-score table for
quizzes, or the mock-test table for mocks, and update the YAML front-matter and status/checkboxes per
the instructions in that file — don't delete history). Give me the updated tracker text to paste in.

Then tell me my weak areas to review next based on these results.
```

**Attach these files:**
- `Context/Progress_Tracker.md`
