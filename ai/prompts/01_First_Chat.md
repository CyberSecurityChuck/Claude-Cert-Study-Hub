# 01 — First Chat (very first session)

**When to use:** Your very first study session, before you've learned anything. This kicks off the whole
program at Step 0.

**Copy-paste prompt:**

```
Please act as my tutor and exam coach following the rules in System_Prompt/AI_System_Prompt.md (I've
attached it). I'm a COMPLETE AI beginner on the free Claude tier — assume I know nothing about AI or
Claude and define every term in plain language.

We are starting from the very beginning. START at Knowledge/00_AI_and_Claude_101_Primer.md (this is
Step 0 — the warm-up). Teach it to me ONE part at a time: give me a short, simple explanation of one
part, use a concrete example or cybersecurity analogy, then pause and let me ask questions or say
"next" before moving on. Do NOT dump the whole file at once.

Tell me clearly whenever a major section is complete, and at the end of Step 0 tell me what comes next.
When we finish or I say to stop, update Context/Progress_Tracker.md (give me the text to paste) with my
last completed section and what's next.
```

- `Context/Progress_Tracker.md`
