# Study Plan — From Today to Exam Day

**Today's date:** 2026-08-25
**Mock-test phase begins:** 2026-09-14
**Deadline to pass:** 2026-09-30

This plan is **flexible and resumable**. You WILL take breaks and pauses — that's expected and fine.
If you miss a day, just shift the plan forward using the buffer/rest days. Your `Progress_Tracker.md`
always tells you (and any AI) where to pick back up.

Study order follows the **official domain weights** (heaviest first), because that's how the exam is
weighted.

---

## The two phases at a glance

- **Phase 1 — Foundations Learning:** ~2–3 weeks (Aug 25 → Sep 13). Learn the material domain by domain.
  End each section with a **5–10 question MCQ checkpoint** (one question at a time, with explanations).
- **Phase 2 — Daily Mock Tests:** Sep 14 → Sep 30. One **full 60-question timed mock** per day, review
  weak areas, repeat. Rest days built in.

## PHASE 2 — Daily Mock Tests (Sep 14 – Sep 30)

From **Sep 14**, switch the AI into mock-test mode (see the Phase 2 kickoff in
`System_Prompt/Handoff_Instructions.md`). Each mock:

- **60 questions**, mixed **multiple choice + multiple response**.
- Questions spread across domains by weight (~16 / 12 / 12 / 11 / 9).
- **120-minute** time limit, **one question at a time**, no answers revealed mid-test.
- Afterward: score out of 60, **estimated scaled score** (pass = **720**), a **PDF-ready question bank**
