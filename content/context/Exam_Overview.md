## What the exam is (in plain English)

The **Claude Certified Architect – Foundations** exam checks that you can **scope and design real
production solutions built on Claude**. In beginner terms, that means proving you can:

- Choose the **right Claude model and deployment platform** for a job (like picking the right tier/SKU
  and hosting for a security tool).
- Decide when to use an **"agentic" architecture** (an AI that can take multiple steps and use tools on
  its own) versus a **"single-shot"** call (one prompt in, one answer out).
- Account for **evaluation** (how you test the AI is good enough), **cost**, and **responsible/safe
  deployment** (guardrails — similar in spirit to security controls you already know).

- **Role:** Architect
- **Level:** Foundations (entry level for this role)

### About "multiple response" questions
Some questions ask you to **select every correct option**, not just one. These are usually all-or-nothing
(you must get every correct choice and no wrong ones). The practice quizzes in this kit include these so
they won't surprise you.

### About the scaled score (100–1000, pass = 720)
Your raw number of correct answers is converted onto a 100–1000 scale, and **720 is the pass mark**. It is
**not** a simple percentage, so don't assume "72% correct = pass." In this kit's mock tests, any scaled
number is an **estimate only** to give you a rough sense of readiness.
